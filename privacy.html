Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Pre-launch notice. Glowe Play has not launched yet and is not currently collecting any information about children — no photos, voice recordings, birthdays, drawings, or stories. This policy covers only our marketing website and email waitlist. Before the product begins collecting any family or child information, we will publish an updated policy and complete a thorough legal review.

1. Who we are

Glowe Play (“we,” “us,” “our”) is an upcoming birthday-tradition keepsake product. You can reach us anytime at hello@gloweplay.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your email address, when you choose to join our waitlist.
  • Basic, aggregate usage data (such as page views and general device/browser type) if and when we enable privacy-conscious analytics. We do not use this to identify you personally, and we do not collect information about children.

We do not knowingly collect information from children through this website.

3. Why we use it

We use your email address only to:

  • Notify you when early access and enrollment open;
  • Share launch details, founding-family pricing, and occasional product updates.

We will not send you unrelated marketing, and we will not spam you.

4. How we store and share it

Your email is stored with the email/waitlist service provider we use to operate the list. We share your email only with service providers who help us run the waitlist, and only for that purpose. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • You can ask us to delete your email from our list by writing to hello@gloweplay.com.

6. Cookies

This pre-launch site uses only what is necessary to function. If we add analytics, we will use a privacy-conscious approach and update this policy.

7. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for parents and adults. We are not collecting children’s personal information at this stage. When Glowe Play begins offering the product, we will provide a separate, detailed explanation of how family and child content is handled, with appropriate protections and legal review (including applicable children’s-privacy laws).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product develops. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at hello@gloweplay.com.
