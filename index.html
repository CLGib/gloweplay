09 — Questions

Good things to ask.

Is this physical, digital, or both?
Both — and the physical part leads. It starts with a welcome kit on your doorstep. A simple, private digital space lets you save photos, drawings, answers, and voice memories through the year. At your child's next birthday, it all becomes a hardcover book.
What do I receive right away?
Your welcome kit ships to your door — the Adventure Passport, badge stickers, memory pouch, door hanger, welcome guide, and a few surprise extras. Everything you need to begin on day one.
Do I need to keep up with an app?
No. There's a private digital space you open only when you want to save a moment — the technology stays in the background; your family stays at the center.
How often do we do an adventure, and how long do they take?
As often as you like — there's no schedule and no pressure. Most adventures take minutes, not hours: a rainbow hunt, a batch of cookies, a bedtime question. The point is time together, not a big production.
How are voice memories added?
Right from your phone in the private digital space — tap record and it's saved to your child's year. A QR code in the finished book plays it back, so you can hear exactly how they sounded that year.
Does AI write my child's story? Can I edit it?
No. Glowe Play is hand-curated, not automated. Your book is built from your family's own words, photos, and drawings — the real things your child said and made. You review and approve everything before it's printed.
Who owns our photos and recordings?
You do. Your family's memories belong to your family — private by default, never sold, and yours to export or delete anytime. Our privacy policy will spell this out in full before we start collecting anything.
What's included, and is the book in the price?
Yes. It's $99 per child, per year, and that includes the starter box (welcome kit), a full year of adventures and memory collection, one printed hardcover book, and standard U.S. shipping.
How much is a second child, or an extra book?
Each child gets their own Adventure Year, their own adventures, and their own hardcover book at $99 per child, per year — so every kid ends up with a shelf that's truly theirs. Want extra copies for grandparents or a co-parent? Additional printed books are $49.95 each.
Does membership renew automatically?
Paid enrollment isn't open yet, so no one is charged during the waitlist. When enrollment opens, we'll show renewal terms clearly before you ever join — you'll always be in control.
What if we miss several months?
No problem, and no penalty. Your book celebrates what you actually did — a lighter year is still a beautiful book. You can pick right back up whenever life allows.
Can separated or divorced parents collaborate privately?
Yes. You control who's invited to your child's year — grandparents, co-parents, anyone you choose — and collaboration can stay private between households.
Is it suitable for children with disabilities or limited speech?
Yes. A child's year can be captured through photos, drawings, favorite things, and a parent's own reflections — never dependent on speech. Every child's story deserves to be told.
When does it launch?
We haven't set a public date yet. Join the waitlist and founding families will be the first to hear — with early access and launch pricing.
