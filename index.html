09 — Questions

Good things to ask.

Can I add multiple children?
Yes. Each child gets their own year, their own adventures, and their own book — so every kid ends up with a shelf that's truly theirs.
What if we miss an adventure?
There's no pressure and no penalty. The adventures are a menu, not a checklist — do the ones that fit your year, and the book celebrates what you actually did together.
How many adventures are there?
Dozens, across every season — from rainbow hunts to backyard campouts to acts of kindness. New ones are added throughout the year.
Can grandparents participate?
Absolutely. Grandparents (and anyone you invite) can add photos, voice memories, and notes to your child's year.
Can I buy previous years?
Starting a little late is completely fine. You can begin with this year and, when we launch, build backward to capture the ages you've already lived.
What ages is this best for?
It shines from the toddler years through the tween years — any age where a child is growing and changing faster than you can hold on to.
Can I reorder books?
Yes — order extra hardcover copies anytime for grandparents, co-parents, or keepsakes.
